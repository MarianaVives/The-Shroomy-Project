Amanita Muscaria

Fly Agaric - Wild Food UKAmanita muscaria, or fly agaric, is a distinctive, iconic mushroom with a bright red or orange cap covered in white spots, white gills, a white stem with a ring, and a bulbous base, known for its psychoactive properties,and traditional use as an insecticide and entheogen, forming symbiotic relationships with trees. It's a large, recognizable fungus, often considered poisonous but rarely deadly, found across Northern Hemisphere temperate/boreal forests and naturalized globally.

Lentinula edodes

Shiitake mushrooms (Lentinula edodes) are popular East Asian fungi prized for their rich, earthy, umami flavor and meaty texture, commonly used in stir-fries, soups, and broths, with both fresh and dried forms available, the latter offering intensified flavor when rehydrated for dishes like risottos or steaming fish. Known for high protein and low fat, they're considered nutritious and a culinary staple, often called the "king of mushrooms".

Lion's Mane

Lion's Mane mushroom (Hericium erinaceus) is a large, shaggy, white fungus resembling a lion's mane or pom-pom, with cascading spines instead of gills, growing on dead or dying hardwood trees. Known for its seafood-like taste (crab/lobster) and meaty texture, it's used culinarily and in traditional Asian medicine for potential cognitive, gut, and immune support, appearing as powders, capsules, or fresh in food.
